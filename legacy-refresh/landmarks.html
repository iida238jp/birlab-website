Landmarks are not a ranking of projects. They trace recurring scientific questions and the experimental systems used to investigate them.

01

Locomotion & morphology

Question: What can body dynamics contribute to efficient movement? Compliant and underactuated walking and hopping platforms investigated behaviour distributed between mechanics and control.

02

Sensing through body dynamics

Core idea: Perception can arise through the coupling of sensing, action, and a physical body. This question establishes an intellectual bridge from locomotion to embodied sensing.

Related project →
03

Physical robot evolution

Question: Can machines participate in the design and fabrication of new physical forms? Robot-evolution platforms made morphology itself an experimental variable.

Representative experiment →
04

Automated physical experimentation

Core idea: A robot can perform, measure, and iterate physical experiments at scale. RoboScientist turned interaction with the material world into a method of discovery.

Representative experiment →
05

Adaptive manipulation

Question: How can mechanics, touch, and control cooperate during uncertain contact? Robotic hands, CambridgeARM, Vegebot, and Foodly provide complementary experimental systems.

Related robots →Related papers →
06

EIT & embodied sensing

Core idea: A deformable body can become a distributed sensing medium. EIT integrates morphology and perception across continuous soft structures.
