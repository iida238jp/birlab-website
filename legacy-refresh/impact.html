Impact / Science to Society

Principle → technology
→ impact.

Impact begins with a scientific principle, develops through an experimental system, and reaches society through collaboration, demonstration, and deployment. We describe that chain without overstating commercial outcomes.

01

Food & Agriculture

Principle: compliant bodies and embodied sensing support interaction with variable, delicate objects. Technology: Vegebot, Foodly, adaptive hands, and agri-food robotics. Activity: field testing and doctoral training through AgriFoRwArdS.

02

Healthcare & Human Support

Principle: physical interaction can guide learning and reveal information. Technology: haptic interaction and safe contact systems. Programme evidence: the completed RoboPatient project investigated robot-assisted learning of constrained haptic information gain.

03

Manufacturing & Automation

Principle: adaptable mechanics and touch support work in less constrained environments. Technology: reconfigurable grippers, tactile arrays, and flexible manipulation. Activity: the confirmed FABROB project addressed flexible robotic prefabrication.

Infrastructure & Future Physical Systems
