Understanding intelligence by building robots.

BIRL investigates how intelligent behaviour emerges through interactions between bodies, materials and environments. We build bio-inspired robots both as useful machines and as experimental systems for understanding embodied intelligence.

The idea behind BIRL

The idea behind BIRL

A principle that has guided BIRL for two decades.

Our research starts from a simple idea: intelligent behaviour is not generated by the brain or controller alone. It emerges through continuous interaction between control, body dynamics, sensing and the environment.

First articulated in our early embodied-intelligence research and highlighted in Science in 2007, this view continues to shape BIRL research today—from locomotion and soft robotics to tactile sensing, physical reservoir computing and dexterous manipulation.
