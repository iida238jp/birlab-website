Science / Big Question

Intelligence emerges
through interaction.

How does intelligence emerge from physical interaction? We build robots to understand intelligence—not only as useful machines, but as experimental systems in which scientific ideas can be made physical and tested.

BIRL studies how morphology, materials, sensing, control, action, and the environment interact to generate adaptive and goal-directed behaviour.

01

Embodied Intelligence

Intelligence is shaped by the body that acts. We investigate how physical form and dynamics participate in behaviour rather than serving only as hardware for computation.

02

Physical Interaction

Contact is not merely a disturbance to control. It can become information: deformation, force, compliance, and environmental response help a system perceive and decide.

03

Emergence & Adaptation

We ask how complex, purposeful behaviour can emerge from local interactions among mechanics, sensing, control, and an environment—and how that behaviour can adapt.

04

Making questions physical

Soft robots, EIT sensing systems, adaptive hands, locomotion platforms, and robotic experiments are ways to isolate, test, and connect these principles.
