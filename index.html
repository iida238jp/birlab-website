02 — Current Research Platforms

Questions made
physical.

Soft Robotics, EIT sensing, and manipulation are experimental platforms within a broader scientific programme: understanding how bodies, materials, sensing, control, action, and environments generate adaptive behaviour.

01

Materials / Morphology / Control

Soft
Robotics

Soft robotic bodies let us investigate how morphology and physical dynamics can participate directly in sensing, control, and adaptation.

02

Turning the body itself into a sensor

EIT Soft
Sensing

We use Electrical Impedance Tomography to read contact and deformation across soft bodies, integrating morphology and perception rather than treating sensing as discrete components.

03

Contact / Mechanics / Adaptation

Soft Robot
Manipulation

Contact-rich manipulation reveals how passive mechanics, tactile feedback, action, and adaptation combine to produce purposeful behaviour in uncertain environments.
